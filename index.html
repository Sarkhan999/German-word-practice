Guess the article from the word

These patterns cover a lot of nouns. Learn them once and many articles stop being a guess.

Habits that help

  • Never learn a noun alone. Learn der Hund, die Hunde as one unit: article, word and plural.
  • Picture the colour. Blue for der, red for die, green for das. Imagine a blue dog, a red cat, a green house.
  • Every German noun starts with a capital letter. If it is capitalised mid-sentence, it is a noun.
  • In a normal sentence the verb comes second: Heute gehe ich nach Hause.
  • Ten new words a day plus a daily review beats fifty words once a week. Practise when the Due counter is above zero.
  • Say every word out loud. Use the Listen button and copy the sound.
  • No umlaut keys? Type ae, oe, ue and ss. The trainer accepts both.
